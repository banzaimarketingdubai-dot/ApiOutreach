# REVO Master Data: Outreach Campaigns & Drip Funnels Strategy

## 1. Core Philosophy & Value Proposition
The outreach engine transforms scraped and enriched leads into paying customers for **GBPilot** (the AI Google Maps Growth Copilot). 

**Key Principles:**
- **Value First, Sell Second:** We do not pitch widgets or generic SEO. We offer a free, personalized 3x3 Geo-Grid analysis report generated by GBPilot.
- **The "Low-Hanging Fruit":** We shift the focus from "views and clicks" to **ready-to-buy, free clients**.
- **AI Search Engine Paradigm:** We educate the user that Google Maps is the primary data source for AI search engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity. If they aren't in the top 3 on Maps, they don't exist in the AI era.
- **Empathy:** We acknowledge their efforts ("You provide great service") and contrast it with their problem ("But you're losing clients because of this technicality").
- **The Core Objection (Continuous Marathon):** We proactively crush the objection that "GBP is a one-time setup." We educate them that Google algorithms decay old posts in 7 days, competitors constantly steal ranks, and malicious edits happen anytime. GBPilot is a continuous 24/7 defender, not a one-time wizard.
- **Frictionless CTA + Direct Links:** Every cold email contains a direct link to the platform (or their personalized audit), but heavily incentivizes them to **reply** to the email to receive the `Welcome14` promo code. Replying builds our domain reputation, while the link captures impatient buyers.
- **Unsubscribe / Opt-Out:** To protect domain reputation and avoid spam traps, EVERY email (both Cold and Hot sequences) must contain a clear, 1-click `[Unsubscribe]` option at the bottom. If clicked, the lead's `is_unsubscribed` flag is set to True, and all sequences instantly halt.

## 2. Dynamic Audience Segmentation
Leads are automatically routed into 3 distinct funnels based on their Apify metrics:

### Segment 1: "Hidden Gems" (High Rating, Low Volume)
- **Criteria:** Rating >= 4.7, Reviews Count < 50.
- **Pain Point:** They provide excellent service, but lack visibility. Competitors with worse service but better profiles are stealing their clients.

### Segment 2: "Sinking Giants" (High Volume, Bad Rating)
- **Criteria:** Reviews Count > 50, Rating < 4.2 (or Low Revo Score).
- **Pain Point:** They are burning their own traffic. People see their profile, see the bad rating, and immediately go to competitors.

### Segment 3: "The Ghosts" (No Profile or Unclaimed)
- **Criteria:** `is_claimed` is false, or no GBP data found.
- **Pain Point:** They literally do not exist for 80% of local searchers. They are missing out on the absolute baseline of modern local commerce.

## 3. The 5-Touch Drip Sequence (Architecture)
The funnel automatically stops the moment a lead **replies** to any email. 

### Touch 1: The Icebreaker (Day 1)
- **Angle:** Empathy + Reality Check + Free Audit Link.
- **Content:** "You have a great service, but you're losing clients to competitors. I ran a GBP analysis for you: [Link]. Here are 2 things you must fix today."
- **Objection Handler:** "Google Maps isn't set-and-forget. It requires continuous updates to stay on top."
- **CTA:** "Click [Here] to try our AI Copilot. Or, reply to this email and I'll send you a `Welcome14` promo code for 14 days of free Autopilot."

### Touch 2: The AI Secret (Day 3)
- **Angle:** FOMO + Global Search Paradigm.
- **Content:** "Did you know ChatGPT and Perplexity pull local recommendations directly from Google Maps Top-3? If you aren't there, you are invisible to the AI future. This isn't about views, it's about free, ready-to-buy clients."
- **CTA:** "Grab your free clients: [App Link]. Reply 'Want' and I'll send you the promo code."

### Touch 3: The Value Drop (Day 7)
- **Angle:** Pure value + The Marathon concept.
- **Content:** "Here is a free hack: Rename your facade photos to include your city and service (e.g., *massage-kyiv.jpg*). But remember, Google's algorithm favors *freshness*. If you stop posting or replying for a week, competitors will push you down."
- **CTA:** "GBPilot generates fresh SEO posts automatically every week. Try it here: [App Link] or reply for the promo code."

### Touch 4: The Math of Lost Profit (Day 14)
- **Angle:** Logical ROI calculation.
- **Content:** "Your competitor gets ~150 free clicks from Maps monthly. At a 10% conversion rate, that's 15 paying clients for free. You are leaving money on the table every single day you don't actively manage your profile."
- **CTA:** "Launch in 2 minutes: [App Link]. Reply to this email to get your free clients back with a promo code."

### Touch 5: The Break-Up (Day 21)
- **Angle:** Loss aversion.
- **Content:** "It seems now isn't a good time for you to scale. I'm closing your analysis file. Remember, your competitors are using AI 24/7 to push your ranking down. If you ever want to fight back, you know where to find me."
- **CTA:** "Final link to access GBPilot: [App Link]."

## 4. UI/UX Interface & Platform Integration
To make this work seamlessly within the existing REVO Master Data (ApiOutreach) platform, we will build the following interfaces:

### A. The Campaign Builder Modal (Front-End)
Accessible via the `Campaign Builder` nav link.
1. **Audience Filter:** Select leads from the Master Data Grid (by Geo, Niche, or pre-built Segments like "Hidden Gems").
2. **Sequence Editor:** A visual timeline where the user can customize the prompts for Touch 1 through 5. 
3. **LLM Prompt Context:** A text area to provide Gemini with the exact product pitch, so Gemini can weave the lead's specific `company_name` and `rating` into the predefined Touch templates.

### B. The Outreach Tracker (Master Data Grid Extension)
- Add new columns to the main table:
  - `Outreach Status`: Badges for `Not Sent`, `In Sequence`, `Replied (Hot!)`, `Bounced`.
  - `Current Touch`: Which step of the funnel they are on (e.g., `Touch 3`).
- Add a "Drafts Review" drawer (similar to the Lead Inspect drawer) where the user can read the Gemini-generated emails and click "Approve & Send".

### C. Backend Architecture (FastAPI + Celery + PostgreSQL)
1. **Database Schema Additions:**
   - `Campaigns` table: Stores audience filters and LLM prompts.
   - `EmailSequences` table: Tracks `lead_id`, `touch_level`, `next_send_date`, `status` (queued, sent, replied).
2. **LLM Mass Generation:**
   - A new Celery worker (`generate_outreach_drafts`) that loops through selected leads, passes the Touch 1-5 templates + Lead Data to Gemini, and saves the results as `Drafts`.
3. **Delivery Engine (Resend/SendGrid):**
   - A cron-based Celery beat task (`process_outreach_queue`) that runs every 5 minutes. It finds approved drafts where `next_send_date <= NOW()`, and sends them via the Resend API (rate-limited to 2-3 per minute).
4. **Webhook Listener:**
   - An API endpoint `/api/webhooks/email-replies` that listens to Resend/SendGrid. If a reply is detected, it finds the lead, updates `status = 'replied'`, halts the sequence, and triggers the `Welcome14` auto-reply email.

## 5. Post-Signup Onboarding & Monetization (Hot Leads Funnel)
Once a user registers (or replies and receives the promo code), they are moved to the **Promo Track** pipeline.

### The Hybrid Freemium Cap (Product-Led Monetization)
We DO NOT offer unlimited automation for 14 days. Instead, we offer a **Usage-Capped Trial**:
- The user has access to full AI capabilities (Pro Tier).
- **Limit:** AI can only reply to **5 reviews** and generate **2 Google Posts**.
- **The "Aha" Trap:** They press `⚡ Approve All` and see the magic happen instantly. They get addicted to the convenience. On the 6th review, they hit the paywall. This creates massive motivation to upgrade because they already experienced the saved time and don't want to go back to manual work.

### The 14-Day Onboarding Sequence (Promo Track)
*Triggered when a Webhook confirms signup.*
- **Day 1: "The Matrix of Truth"** -> "Check your 3x3 Geo-Grid. See the red zones? That's where competitors are stealing your clients."
- **Day 3: "Invisible Shield"** -> "Did you know competitors can suggest edits to your hours? Turn on **Profile Guard** to auto-revert malicious edits 24/7."
- **Day 7: "The AI Autopilot"** -> "Check your FTC Speedometer. Notice you have 0 Pending unreplied reviews? Our AI handled them all this week. GBP is a continuous marathon, not a sprint."
- **Day 11: "Magic Button Habit"** -> "Don't forget to click `⚡ Approve All` today. Fresh content tells Google you are active, pushing you higher."
- **Day 14: "The Choice" (Paywall)** -> "Your trial is over. Your AI is sleeping, and Profile Guard is off. Upgrade to Starter to reactivate your 24/7 employee, or risk losing rankings to competitors who use AI."
